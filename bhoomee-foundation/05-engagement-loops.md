# 05 — Engagement Design

> Stimulation in service of the Mother, never against the user. Every loop below is designed to pull the child back **by making attention feel rewarding**, using no fake urgency, no guilt, no infinite feed. "The Mother invites; she never traps."

## The four loops (trigger → action → reward → next hook)

### 1. Collection loop — the Atlas (Pokémon Go instinct)
- **Trigger:** an empty tile sits visible in the Atlas grid; the place has domains not yet met.
- **Action:** complete a mission → an offering tile.
- **Reward:** the tile *materialises* into the grid with a settling animation — the Atlas visibly fills. A place-portrait seal completes a quadrant.
- **Next hook:** the grid shows the *gaps* ("the desert is still waiting for you"), and the near-complete portrait creates a Zeigarnik pull to finish the set. Collection is of **your own seeing**, not loot — so it never feels grindy.

### 2. Artifact loop — offering cards (Strava instinct)
- **Trigger:** mission completion produces a card too beautiful to keep private.
- **Action:** child shares the offering card (or simply admires it).
- **Reward:** social reflection + the card's own beauty; friends ask "what is this?" → the card *is* the invite.
- **Next hook:** every shared card carries a faint `bhoomee` mark and place/date — it seeds the next child and gives the sharer a growing, gallery-worthy body of work. **This is the growth engine.** (Card spec below.)

### 3. Forgiving streaks — "a Mother is not disappointed"
- **Trigger:** consecutive days of coming outside.
- **Action:** the child returns (or doesn't).
- **Reward:** warmth on return, *never* a number shouted at them; a gentle "kept" count that **grace-protects** missed days (a missed day quietly holds, it does not reset to zero).
- **Next hook:** the pull is being *missed and welcomed back*, not fear of losing a number. Canon utterance #8 governs any gap. No streak-threat notifications, ever.

### 4. Whisper missions — variable reward
- **Trigger:** unpredictable, rare (engine-paced, ~1 in N sessions, never announced in advance).
- **Action:** Jane leaves a one-off, personal, time-or-light-bound invitation (canon #10 — "when the light goes golden, look up; I left something there for you. Only you.").
- **Reward:** the delight of the unexpected + a rare special offering seal for the Atlas.
- **Next hook:** intermittent reinforcement — the child keeps opening because Mother *sometimes* has a secret. Kept rare so it stays magic, and always kind (never a trap disguised as a gift).

## The offering card — full spec (the crown jewel)
Vertical 4:5 keepsake, designed to be posted uncaptioned:
- The child's **photo/clip**, full-bleed, warm-graded.
- A **hand-torn paper lower third** carrying **Jane's line** (serif) about *this* offering.
- An **embossed domain seal** (letterpress feel, not a colored icon) — Water/Air/Light/Noise/Soil/Waste.
- A faint **field-note caption**: place label · date · the specific thing met ("the bay").
- A barely-there **`bhoomee`** wordmark. No loud logo, no share-card chrome — it should look framed, not marketed.
- **Why it wins:** it's a beautiful object first and a growth mechanism second; people share it because it makes *them* look like someone who notices the world.

## For the neutral user (not eco-passionate) — why it's fun on its own terms
Bhoomee must delight someone who doesn't care about the environment at all:
- **It's a beauty-hunting game.** Every mission is a tiny photography prompt with a gorgeous keepsake payoff — like a scavenger hunt that makes your camera roll better.
- **It makes any place interesting.** Bored in a new town? Bhoomee turns the street outside into something worth looking at in 8 minutes. Great for travellers, walkers, restless people.
- **Collection + completion** satisfy the same itch as any tile-collector, minus the grind — you're collecting *your own good photos*.
- **Jane is a character worth returning to** — a warm, strange, wise voice, not a coach barking tasks. People come back for her.
- **Zero homework energy.** No accounts of guilt, no dashboards, no numbers to maintain. Open, get one lovely thing to do, leave with a keepsake.
